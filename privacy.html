Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Granted Industries LLC ("Granted Industries," "we," "us") operates the website at grantedindustries.com. This policy explains what information we receive when you visit the site or contact us, and what we do with it.

The short version

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal information. The only personal information we receive directly is what you choose to send us by email.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the contents of your message.
  • Technical information. As with most websites, the servers that deliver this site automatically receive basic technical details from your browser, such as your IP address, browser type, and the time of your request. This goes to the service providers listed below rather than to us directly.

This site has no accounts, forms, or payments, and it does not set cookies.

How we use information

  • To respond to your messages and provide support.
  • To keep the site secure and working.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of providers to run this site and our email. Each handles information under its own privacy terms.

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests the font files from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Workspace provides our email, so messages you send us are processed and stored by Google on our behalf.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of the business.

Our apps

We are building mobile applications. This policy currently covers this website only. Before any app becomes available, we will update this policy or publish a separate one describing what that app collects and why.

Retention

We keep email correspondence for as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain our business records. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing support@grantedindustries.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor requests as that law requires.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version here and revise the effective date above.
